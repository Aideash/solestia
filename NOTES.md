Because we use apparent solar time rather than a uniform count, Mercury's hand genuinely creeps backwards for a few days around perihelion — its Sun reverses in the sky. I left that in as real behaviour; switching to a mean Sun is a one-line change in localSolarTime if it reads as a glitch to you.
